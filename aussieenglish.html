AUSSIE ENGLISH

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Aussie English mobile application.

Effective date: 4 October 2026

1. About This Policy

Aussie English is an educational vocabulary application developed by Mert Selvi. It helps users learn and compare Australian and British English through word challenges, study cards and memory matching activities.

By using Aussie English, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information You Provide

Aussie English does not require an account, registration or login. The app does not ask you to provide your name, postal address, telephone number, date of birth or payment information.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message. This information is used only to respond to your request, provide support and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores certain gameplay information locally on your device so that your progress can continue between sessions. This may include:

  • Game progress and unlocked levels
  • Scores, streaks and completed challenges
  • Study-card position and preferred card direction
  • Selected difficulty and gameplay preferences
  • Whether the introductory screens have been completed

This locally stored progress is not transmitted to the developer. You can remove it by deleting the app from your device.

4. Advertising and Automatically Collected Data

Aussie English uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location
  • Device identifiers, including an advertising identifier where available and permitted
  • Advertisements viewed and interactions with advertisements
  • App interactions such as launches, taps and views
  • Crash logs, diagnostic information and performance data
  • Device and operating-system information

This information may be used for advertisement delivery, frequency control, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Advertising may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your region, device settings, consent choices and applicable law.

The developer may receive aggregated advertising reports from AdMob. These reports are intended to measure advertisement performance and are not used by the developer to identify you personally.

5. Third-Party Services

Google processes advertising-related information under its own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google My Ad Center

Third-party advertisements may link to external websites or services. We do not control the privacy practices or content of those external services.

6. Permissions and Device Features

Aussie English does not request access to your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo library or health data.

Approximate location may still be inferred by Google from an IP address as part of advertising and security operations.

7. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining app functionality
  • Saving gameplay progress locally
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements
  • Improving reliability and diagnosing technical problems
  • Preventing fraud, abuse and invalid advertising activity
  • Responding to support and privacy enquiries
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations

8. Legal Bases and Regional Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, the processing of information may rely on your consent, legitimate interests, the provision of requested services or compliance with legal obligations.

Users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland and other applicable regions may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, and may have the right to withdraw consent.

Residents of applicable United States jurisdictions may also have rights concerning access, deletion, correction and certain uses or disclosures of personal information.

To submit a request concerning information controlled directly by the developer, contact us using the email address below. Requests concerning information processed independently by Google should also be directed through Google's privacy controls.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally remains on your device until you delete the app or the information is otherwise cleared from the device.

Support emails may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, maintain support records or comply with legal obligations.

Google retains advertising-related data according to its own policies, legal obligations and account settings.

10. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers such as Google may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, such processing is subject to appropriate legal safeguards described in the provider's privacy documentation.

11. Children's Privacy

Aussie English is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly to the developer.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

12. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information. However, no electronic transmission, advertising service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, advertising services, legal requirements or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.

14. Your Choices

  • You can remove locally stored gameplay progress by deleting the app.
  • You can manage certain advertising and tracking preferences through your iOS privacy settings.
  • You can manage Google advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center.
  • Where a consent or privacy-options screen is presented, you can use it to manage the choices available in your region.
The developer does not sell personal information directly and does not operate a user-data marketplace. Advertising providers may process information as described in their own policies and as permitted by your settings, consent choices and applicable law.
